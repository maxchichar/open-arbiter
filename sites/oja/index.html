Why you can trust the price

  • The arbiter at … is open-source deterministic code. Same inputs, same price, every time.
  • Price = midpoint between the seller's sealed floor and the lower of your maximum and the list price, on a price step of 2.5% of the list price (at least ₦100 per unit).
  • Neither limit is ever returned or logged in public. An agreed price narrows each limit to one price step, however many deals are made; "no deal" says only that there was no overlap. Your AI provider sees what you tell the assistant.
  • Rebidding the same terms with a new maximum is refused, so no one can map a seller's floor.
  • Every step is written to a hash-chained audit log, and receipts are signed with ECDSA P-256 and checked against the key pinned in this site (arbiter's published key).
  • Other agents can use it too, over MCP at …, or by calling this site's network.
