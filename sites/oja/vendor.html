An agreed price only shows which price step (₦100, ₦200, ₦400 … per unit, between 1.25% and 2.5% of your list price) your lowest price falls in. Your lowest price cannot fall inside the partial step just under your list price; if it does, the arbiter tells you the highest it accepts.
